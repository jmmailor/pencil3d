Pencil3D

Draw in 3D with a pencil. Lines stay in place over the camera view and export to Rhino.

  1. Print the desk sheet and the pencil cube (print page) at 100%.
  2. Put the sheet on the desk and the phone on a stand looking down at it at an angle.
  3. Put the cube on the end of the pencil and calibrate the tip once (Setup).
  4. Hold the draw button while you move the pencil.

Calibrate tip

Put the pencil tip on the ○ at the centre of the sheet. Keep the tip still and tilt the pencil around it in a circle (about 30°).
