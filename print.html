Print markers

Print both pages at 100% / actual size (turn off "fit to page"). Then measure the black squares with a ruler: the sheet's must be exactly 3.0 cm.

  1. Lay the desk sheet flat (tape it down). The text edge faces you.
  2. For a sturdier cube, glue the print onto thin card first. Cut it out, fold along the dashed lines with the print outside, and glue the tabs.
  3. Push the pencil's eraser end into the hole in the bottom so the pencil sits straight under the cube's centre. Then measure the pencil length from the cube's bottom face to the tip.
  4. In the app: Setup → set the same paper, cube size and pencil length, then Calibrate tip.

A bigger cube or a shorter pencil gives better accuracy. Matte paper avoids reflections.
